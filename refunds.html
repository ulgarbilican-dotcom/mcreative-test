Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated: [DATE]

1. Monthly packages

Our packages are services that we start working on right away: planning, shooting, editing and posting. Because of that, a month that has started is not refunded. You can cancel for the next month at any time, as explained in the terms, and you will not be charged again.

2. If something goes wrong

If we did not deliver what was agreed for the month, tell us within 14 days at hello@mcreativestudio.be. We first try to put it right (redo the work or make up the missing content). If that is not possible, we refund the part of the month that was not delivered.

3. Custom quotes and one-off projects

The refund and payment terms of a custom quote are stated in the quote itself. Work that has already been done, and costs that we have already paid to third parties for you (for example a location, a model or a licence), are not refundable.

4. Right of withdrawal for consumers

If you buy as a consumer (for private purposes) at a distance, for example online, you normally have 14 days to withdraw from the agreement without giving a reason, counted from the day the agreement is made. If you ask us to start the service during this period, you agree to pay for what we have done up to the moment you withdraw, in proportion to the full price. If the service has been fully carried out at your request, and you confirmed that you lose the right of withdrawal, you can no longer withdraw.

To withdraw, send us a clear statement by email to hello@mcreativestudio.be. You can use the model withdrawal form of the European Union, but you do not have to.

This right does not apply to businesses.

5. How refunds are paid

An approved refund is paid back within 14 days, to the account or card you paid with, in the same currency.

6. Questions

Write to hello@mcreativestudio.be or call +32 485 81 41 61. We will always look for a fair solution first.
