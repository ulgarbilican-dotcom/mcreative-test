Frequently asked questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our packages, content, websites and apps, and your data.

General

How fast do you reply?

We reply within two working days. If your question is urgent, say so in the first line of your message.

How do I reach you?

Use the support form. It sends your question straight to us, and it helps us answer faster because we know what it is about.

What should I include in my message?

Your name, your brand and what your question is about. Links, screenshots and examples help us a lot.

Do you work in another language?

We work in English and Dutch. Write to us in the language you prefer.

Packages and invoices

How do the monthly packages work?

A package is billed monthly in advance and renews automatically. You can see what each package includes on the website.

Can I change my package?

Yes. You can move up or down. Tell us through the support form and the change starts from the next month.

How do I cancel?

Send us a message through the support form. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current month. Read the terms and refund policy for the details.

Where do I find my invoice?

Invoices are sent by email. If you cannot find one, send us a message and we will send it again.

Are your prices including VAT?

No. Prices on the website exclude VAT. The VAT of 21% is added on your invoice, unless stated otherwise.

Is the ad budget included?

No. Advertising budgets are paid directly by you to the platform and are not part of our fee.

Can I get a refund?

A month that has started is not refunded. If something was not delivered, we first put it right, and otherwise refund the missing part. Read the refund policy.

Content and delivery

How do I receive my content?

We share your content through a link. If you cannot open it, tell us and we send it another way.

Something is missing from my delivery. What now?

Send us a message within 14 days with what you expected. We check it and make up what is missing.

Can I ask for changes to my content?

Yes. Tell us what you would like to change. Small adjustments are part of the process. Larger changes can be planned in your next month or quoted separately.

Can I use my content everywhere?

Yes, for your own business, once the invoice is paid. Third-party material, such as stock images, fonts and music, follows its own licence. Read the terms.

Will you show my work in your portfolio?

Only finished work, and not if you tell us in writing that you prefer we do not.

Websites and apps

How does a website or app project work?

We start with a call about your goals, then a concept and structure, then design, build, testing and launch. You get a quote with the scope and price before we start.

How long does a website take?

It depends on the size. A small site usually takes a few weeks. We give you a planning with the quote. [CONFIRM TYPICAL DURATION]

What do you need from me?

Your texts, photos, logo and brand colours if you have them, and feedback on time. We can help you create what is missing.

Something on my website is not working. What do I do?

Send us a message with the page, what you expected and what happened. A screenshot and the device and browser you used help us find the cause faster.

Can I change the content myself?

That depends on how your website is built. We tell you in the quote and show you how it works at launch. [CONFIRM WHICH TOOLS YOU BUILD WITH]

Who owns the domain and the hosting?

We recommend that the domain is registered in your name. We explain the options for hosting in the quote, so you always know who pays what and who controls what. [CONFIRM POLICY]

Do you maintain my website after launch?

We can. Maintenance, updates and small changes can be agreed as a monthly package or as work on request. [CONFIRM OFFER AND PRICES]

How many rounds of feedback are included?

The number of feedback rounds is stated in your quote, so there are no surprises. [CONFIRM NUMBER OF ROUNDS]

Will my website work on a phone?

Yes. Every website we build is responsive, which means it adapts to phones, tablets and computers.

Do you build apps for iPhone and Android?

Yes, we build apps. The platforms and features are agreed in the quote.

What if my website goes down?

Tell us right away through the support form and mark it as urgent. We look at the cause first and report back. Outages caused by a hosting or domain provider may need that provider to fix them.

Social media accounts

Whose account is it?

Your accounts stay yours. We only work in them with the access you give us, and you can remove that access at any time.

How do you get access to my accounts?

Through the official ways of each platform, such as adding us as a partner or manager, so you never have to share your password.

A post did not go live. What now?

Send us a message with the platform and the date. Platforms sometimes block or delay posts, and we check what happened and republish where possible.

Can you promise followers or sales?

No. Platforms change their rules and algorithms, and we do not control them. We work with care and skill to make your content as strong as possible. Read the terms.

I lost access to my account. Can you help?

We can guide you through the platform’s recovery steps. We cannot get into an account for you without your permission.

Your data and privacy

What data do you keep about me?

Only what we need to answer you and run your project: contact details, invoices and project files. Read the privacy policy.

Can I see, correct or delete my data?

Yes. Send us a message through the support form and we answer within one month.

Do you use cookies?

No advertising or analytics cookies. Read the cookie policy.
